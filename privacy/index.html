Privacy policy

Back to the site

Kitchen Tactics is made by Tezgah, a one-person studio. This page says exactly what the game stores, where it is stored, and how to have it removed. It is short because the game collects very little, and specific because a policy that is not specific is not worth reading.

Last updated 9 September 2026.

The short version

  • This website stores nothing about you and loads nothing from anyone else — no analytics, no cookies, no embedded trackers.
  • Playing on your own stores your progress on your own device, and on your CrazyGames account if you have one and are signed in.
  • Playing with friends sends your CrazyGames username and account id to our room server, so the other three chairs can see who is at the table.
  • There is no chat, no advertising identifier, no email address, no password and no payment. The game is free and has nothing to sell.

This website

These pages are hand-written HTML, CSS and SVG served as files. They set no cookie, run no analytics and request nothing from any other host — the site's own test suite fails if a single request leaves this origin. The playable build embedded on the Play section runs from this same origin.

Playing on your own

Five things are saved so the game remembers you between visits: your lifetime stats, your run counter, your sound setting, your effects setting and your chosen language. They are stored in your browser on your own device.

When the game runs on CrazyGames, those same five values are also written to the CrazyGames Data module, which stores them against your CrazyGames account so they follow you to another device. That is a CrazyGames facility and their privacy policy governs it; we send it nothing except the five values above. None of it reaches a server of ours. If you are not signed in, it stays on your device alone.

Playing with friends

A multiplayer match runs on our own server, because CrazyGames provides no networking. This is the only part of the game that sends anything about you anywhere.

When you open or join a Room, the game asks CrazyGames for a short-lived signed token identifying you and passes it to that server. The server checks the signature against CrazyGames' own public key and then discards the token — it is removed from the request before the Room itself ever sees it, and it is never written down.

What the Room keeps, for as long as the match lasts, is:

  • your CrazyGames username, shown on your seat so the other players know whose chair is whose;
  • your CrazyGames account id, used to recognise you if your connection drops and you come back to the same chair;
  • the state of the match itself — your board, your gold, your health. Game data, not personal data.

The Room writes that record to its own storage a couple of times a round, so a match survives the server moving it between machines mid-game. Your username is inside that record, because it is the name printed on your seat. Nothing else about you is.

If you are not signed in to CrazyGames, none of this happens. No token is sent, no username and no account id exist to store, and the server names your chair with a plain numbered label. You can play every part of the game this way. The game never asks you to sign in, and never pops up a sign-in prompt on its own.

Where it is stored, and by whom

The room server runs on Cloudflare Workers, one isolated object per room, at kitchen-tactics-rooms.kitchen-tactics-rooms.workers.dev. Cloudflare hosts it; they are our only processor for this data, and they hold it on our behalf under their own terms. If the game later moves to a domain of its own, this hostname changes and this page changes with it.

CrazyGames is the other party involved, and only where this page says so: they issue the token that proves who you are, and they hold the five saved values described above. Their handling of your CrazyGames account is theirs to describe — see crazygames.com/privacy.

What is never collected

No email address, no password, no real name, no profile picture, no location, no device fingerprint, no advertising identifier and no payment details. There is no chat in the game, so nothing you type is sent anywhere — there is nowhere to type. No third-party analytics or tracking service is used anywhere in the game or on this site.

How long it is kept

The five saved values stay until you clear your browser storage, or — on a CrazyGames account — until you remove them through CrazyGames.

A Room's record stays in that room's own storage on Cloudflare. There is no scheduled deletion of it today; that is stated plainly rather than dressed up as a retention policy, and it is the honest description of what the code does. Ask us and it is deleted.

Asking about it, or having it removed

Write to alperdemdev@gmail.com and say which CrazyGames username you are asking about. You can ask what is held, ask for it to be deleted, or object to it being held at all — in which case play the game signed out, and nothing is held in the first place.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects nothing beyond what is described above from anyone. It has no accounts of its own, no purchases and no messaging.

Changes

If what the game stores changes, this page changes in the same commit as the code that changed it, and the date at the top moves. Previous versions are in the game's source history.
